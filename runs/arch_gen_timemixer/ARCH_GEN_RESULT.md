## Verdict

**All three checks pass on every dataset/seed.** The central findings — H1c (causally difficulty-specific features exist), Task G (a learned scorer on them doesn't beat a constant band), and Phase 3 (their natural activation carries no predictive signal) — reproduce on a structurally different (MLP-based, non-attention) architecture. The claim is not an iTransformer artifact.

> No new exploitation mechanism, no layer sweep on the new architecture. This is a generalization check on the existing findings.
