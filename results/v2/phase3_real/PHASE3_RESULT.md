## What this tests (causal-ladder framing)

An **L1 (associational)** test: do the *natural, non-intervened* activations of the L2-validated MVDF features predict `S_t` out-of-sample? A feature found via L2 (dose-response) evidence does not automatically carry L1 predictive information — Elazar et al. 2021 (amnesic probing) is the direct precedent (decodability ≠ the model's computation using it). Four held-out estimators, so 'the MLP wasn't powerful enough' is closed.

## Reading

**No usable natural predictive signal on ETTh1/ETTh2 — marginal or activation-conditioned — across four estimator classes.** The 'your predictor wasn't powerful enough' objection is closed: linear, isotonic, GBM and a pinball MLP all fail. Combined with the synthetic Phase 1 (where the same battery recovered R² 0.26–0.48 on a *designed-in* signal), this is direct evidence that the real ETT representation's L2-validated difficulty features carry little to no L1 natural predictive information — the rung-mismatch the paper diagnoses, measured directly at the feature level, not inferred from downstream conformal failure.

> L1/L2 language discipline (Joshi et al. 2026): these results are associational. Use 'predicts' / 'correlates with' / 'is decodable from', not 'encodes' / 'represents' / 'the mechanism for' — those are reserved for the L2 dose-response results (`P_j`/`U_j`/`R_j`).
