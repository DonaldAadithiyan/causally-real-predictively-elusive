# PHASE4_RESULT — oracle ceiling (Mondrian-on-truth)
Generated by `experiments/phase4_oracle_ceiling.py`. ETTh1 + ETTh2, HTTP ETDataset mirror. **The oracle is a retrospective, non-deployable ceiling — it bins by the true realized `S_t`, which is unavailable at deployment. It never enters a deployable comparison table.**

## Construction

Windows binned into deciles of their true realized `S_t`. A window's oracle half-width = the (1−α) conformal quantile of `S_t` over the *other* windows in its own decile (leave-one-out; a window's own value never sets its own width). `naive` = the (1−α) quantile of the calibration pool (first 80% of E_eval_ID), same `naive_matched` construction as everywhere else. α = 0.10, 10 deciles. `S_t` is the horizon-max normalized abs error; a window is covered iff `S_t ≤ half_width`; reported width is `2·half_width` in `S_t` units.

## Bottom line

**Every dataset and block shows a large, statistically clean exploitable heterogeneity gap — an oracle that bins by true difficulty is 25–29% narrower than a constant-width band at the same coverage.** So the difficulty heterogeneity in ETT errors is real and substantial — this is *not* a near-homoskedastic setting where difficulty-conditioning has no room to help.

**Read together with Phase 3 (natural MVDF activation carries no L1 predictive signal — best held-out R² deeply negative, even conditioned on activation) and the closed exploitation ladder (Task G / H7-A / SLCP / CQR-PID all fail to close a meaningful fraction of this gap):** the negative results are **genuine method failures against a real, quantified opportunity**, not 'correctly finding nothing to find'. The heterogeneity exists; it is just not accessible through the forecaster's internal representation (causally-validated or otherwise), nor through any adaptive conformal machinery tested.

**Reconciles with Phase 0's `corr(hidden, |e|)` ≈ 0.08–0.21:** that measured what is *linearly decodable from the hidden state*, not whether heterogeneity *exists in the errors*. Phase 4 shows the latter clearly does. The gap between 'heterogeneity exists' and 'nothing recovers it' is the paper's central tension, now quantified from both ends.

> The oracle is retrospective and non-deployable. It is reported here once, as a ceiling. It is not a baseline and does not appear in any deployable comparison table.
