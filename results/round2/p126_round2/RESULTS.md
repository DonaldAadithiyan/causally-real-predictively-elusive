## 0. Headline

1. **Predictable difficulty (step zero, §13 rule).** No dataset passes on a valid cell,
   across ETTh1, ETTh2, electricity, traffic, solar, ETTm1 and ETTm2. The rule passes
   formally on traffic ID (2 of 3 seeds), but that cell has 0.22 bootstrap blocks: the "CI"
   is a single point and base coverage is 1.000. ETTh2 s0 and electricity s0 pass a single
   seed each, also on cells with fewer than 1.5 blocks. Every shift cell fails.
2. **Learned scorers vs the constant band, like-for-like, corrected path (ETTh, electricity,
   traffic).** Nothing beats the constant band consistently. The one consistent like-for-like
   winner is **CQR with delayed ACI under shift**, at 9–29% narrower on ETTh/electricity and
   64–67% on traffic. CQR is a quantile model and does not use the representation.
3. **Selection.** On ETTh and synthetic, the type-B enrichment is what the selection
   procedure produces from shuffled or random inputs (0 of 8 runs exceed either null). On
   **electricity it exceeds both nulls in 2 of 3 seeds (p = 0.001)**, and on traffic in 1
   of 3. Effect sizes are 0.5–0.9% of SD(S) on ETTh and electricity and about 4–6% on
   traffic.
4. **Synthetic control on the corrected path.** Link 4 goes from 1/3 to 3/3. Under random
   contiguous blocks, MVDF is 7–13% narrower than `naive_matched`, with bootstrap 90% range
   above 0 on every seed. But dense, PCA and correlated features do the same, and random_k
   does in 2 of 3 seeds, so **the gain is not MVDF-specific**.
5. **The dial: persistence, not noise ratio, governs accessible gains, and the oracle
   tracks neither.**
   - The oracle ratio is 0.31–0.48 in all 15 settings, including ratio 1.0, where the
     noise scale is constant. **The oracle does not measure accessible difficulty**,
     confirming round 1's item B.
   - In distribution, the gain from MVDF is about 0 at p = 0.97, 4–11% at p = 0.995 and
     20–27% at p = 0.999, at *every* noise ratio, including 1.0. The noise ratio adds only
     a modest monotone effect at fixed p.
   - Ratio 1.0 is a noise-scale null, not a difficulty null. The DGP's regime also shifts
     the mean (y = mu_z·1[z=1] + f(x) + σ(z)·η, mu_z = 0.6), so persistent regimes keep
     forecast error predictable.
   - Under shift, MVDF gains are 0–2.5% everywhere.
6. **WP4.** No hidden representation carries a rank signal that survives the circular null.
   No positive control clears the variance-matched null, so the steering test cannot
   distinguish a real direction from a random one. **That is uninformative, not negative.**

**Reading the dial:**
- **Persistence dominates.** At p = 0.97, MVDF gains nothing at any ratio (−0.01 to 0.02).

- **The noise ratio matters only at fixed p, and modestly.** At p = 0.995, MVDF's
  reduction rises 0.04 → 0.06 → 0.08 → 0.09 → 0.11 from ratio 1 to 5.
- **MVDF beats the TRUE-σ ceiling at ratio ≤ 1.5 and p = 0.999.** That ceiling ignores

  0.025 in all 15 settings. gbm_union, from forecast-time features, gets 0.03–0.08,

- **The oracle ratio (0.31–0.48)** rises slightly with p and does not track the TRUE-σ or

- **Implication for the real data.** Accessible width gains on synthetic data need regimes
  that persist over hundreds of windows. The real-data step-zero failure fits difficulty
  regimes that are short-lived relative to the calibration and test segments, but that is
  inferred, not tested.

  n = 8000, not proven.
- That the ratio-1.0 predictability of S_t comes from the regime mean channel (mu_z). The

- That short-lived real-data regimes explain the step-zero failure. This is suggested by
  the dial, not tested.
- That the electricity enrichment survives a fresh SAE. Not tested: Phase 5 found ETTh
  feature identity does not survive reseeding.

## 5. Departures from the brief

- **Synthetic WP3** was run on synth seed 0 and ratio 1.0 seed 0 only, not seeds 1–2.
- **The dial uses seed 0 only**, with an MVDF-only "light" analysis on the 13 non-reference

- **The traffic ID block (1169 windows) is too short** for an embargoed inner GBM

  (200 trees, depth 3, learning rate 0.05) when the inner train/val split is under 100/50
  windows. This affects traffic only.
- **Electricity, traffic, solar and ETTm models were trained on MPS.** Their fingerprints
  match the originals at the data level; the weights are a fresh, saved training run, not
  bit-identical to Kaggle.
- **The brief's sanity criterion** ("within 3 MC SE of 0.90") fails for ACI at short n.
  This is reported, not hidden.
